About the brand
Nutritionist Marina Moreira helps women build food autonomy without restrictive diets, through nutrition care based on active listening and free of judgment, so they can live at peace with food.

The brand's purpose is to give women back the pleasure of eating,
and to take the fear of food out of their minds.

Marina had already had two different logos and never felt they represented her. She felt disconnected. More than ever, it was important to understand who Marina is at heart and what message she wants to share.

The challenge:
Uncover the essence of the brand and communicate it visually, in a strategic way.

Deliverables:

- Brand strategy
- Visual universe

Motion by Raphael Freire

How do we convey a happy, empathetic, welcoming and cozy essence visually?

How do we show the transformation she brings in a minimal, delicate and light way?

How do we stand out from current competitors?
