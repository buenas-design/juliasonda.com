Amor no Feed

Lifestyle · Branding · Packaging · Print & Collateral · Social Media

Amor no Feed is Isa and Rafa's space for couples who want to talk with more presence, on social media and off it. I created the brand, a warm, handwritten identity in olive, terracotta and blush, and then its first product: Nós Dois, a card game for couples.

Sixty cards in three phases: light questions to open up space, deeper reflections without the weight, and small challenges that turn into memories. I designed the cards, the box, the welcome card, the instructions, the launch posts and the landing page.
