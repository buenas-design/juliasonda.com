  • Creative direction
  • Logo direction

  • Brand photography

  • Icons & illustrations

The project

Alasso is a new 284-home community in Golden, Colorado, with apartments, townhomes and shops on the ground floor, a short drive from Denver and close to the trails. Thompson Thrift had already chosen the name, a nod to the gold ore refined in Golden's early days.

The challenge

One brand had to speak to young professionals, Colorado School of Mines students and empty nesters, people who come to Golden for a good job and for the mountains alike. And the palette was already set, a nod to the brewery that calls Golden home.

The idea

We took the gold story one step further. A home isn't built overnight: it forms slowly, like an alloy, through pressure, heat, time and the people around you. That's why the whole brand looks made by hand, from the brushstroke textures to the irregular edges of the letters.

My part

With the team at Zipcode Creative, I wrote the creative direction, directed the logo, and built the visual identity, the 28-page brand guide, the amenity icons and the handmade textures and patterns. I selected the brand photography, too.

The concepts

Four concepts, one choice

I presented four concepts to the client. Concepts 2 and 4 went on to a second round, and they chose Concept 2, Alloy & Echo.

  1. Concept 1 Refined Gold Structured, warm, geometric, elevated, balanced.
  2. Concept 2The chosen one Alloy & Echo Crafted, artisanal, authentic, evolving.
  3. Concept 3 Conductor Dynamic, connected, electric, flowing.
  4. Concept 4 Confluence Layered, sophisticated, convergent, rooted.

The logo

Letters with a handmade edge

My direction asked for a wordmark “in a light serif or a refined sans, with a handcrafted, irregular feel.” I directed the logo through a few rounds, until the letters and the round icon felt as handmade as the rest of the brand.

The final brand

Gold, brick and brushstrokes

Seven colors, from Gold to Amber, with Brick and Merlot for depth. Brushstroke and splatter textures made by hand, two woven patterns and fifteen amenity icons with the same irregular, hand-made strokes.
