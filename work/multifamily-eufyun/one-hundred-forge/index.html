  • Logo
  • Creative direction

  • Brand photography

The project

Woodmont Forge is a 300-home community in Pennington, New Jersey, near Princeton, built in 2024. Living Residential was giving it a new name, One Hundred Forge, and it needed a brand to match.

The challenge

The new name had to feel polished, timeless and welcoming to the professionals and Princeton faculty who live nearby. Nothing industrial, even with Forge in the name, and nothing that shouts luxury. And the slate blue already painted around the property had to stay.

The idea

So the brand grew from the number in its name, and from the idea of completion and balance: shapes that feel resolved and whole, rich heritage colors instead of grays, and a logo where the two O's of FORGE also read as 100.

My part

This one was mine from the first sketch. With the team at Zipcode Creative, I drew the logo, wrote the creative direction, and built the visual identity and the 28-page brand guide. I selected the brand photography, too.

“We like artsy, so be free in creativity! It helps us be memorable.”

What they asked for

“The number 100 represents something that's perfect, established and safe.”On the new name

“Use rich, heritage colors (vibrant), don't use dull colors (neutrals or gray shades)”On color

“Current color is a slate blue that has been activated on the property… important to keep this color in the palette.”On the property

“Avoid industrial style”On style

“Avoid ‘luxury’”On the words

The concepts

Two concepts, one choice

I presented two concepts to the client. They chose Concept 2, On Point, the geometric one.

  1. Concept 1 Artsy Expressive, cultural, creative, welcoming.
  2. Concept 2The chosen one On Point Polished, secure, established, timeless, refined.

The chosen one · Concept 2

On Point

“This direction is rooted in the idea of completion and balance. Everything should feel intentional, resolved, and whole.”

From there I drew the logo: the two O's of FORGE stacked inside a frame, so the name also reads as 100.

Polished · Secure · Established · Timeless · Refined

The logo

Two zeros make a hundred

I filled pages with ways to write 100 before landing on the simplest one: the two O's of FORGE stacked inside a frame, so the name reads as a word and as a number at the same time.

The final brand

Heritage colors, quiet geometry

Seven colors, from Limestone to Warm Ochre, with the slate blue the client asked to keep. Circles, quarter circles and thin grid lines carry the rest, all the way down to the twelve amenity icons.
