The project

Watermark at Jordan Creek is a 176-home community in West Des Moines, Iowa, built in 2015, with stone walls and archways none of its competitors have. BAM Management kept the name and asked for a whole new brand around it.

The challenge

Occupancy had dipped, but the reputation was good. Next to much bigger properties, Watermark had to stand out as the boutique choice, with personal service and a close community, in colors that look good next to the stone.

The idea

We read the name as an identifying mark, not as water. Living here leaves a mark you carry with you, so the brand became a signature: a lowercase wordmark with “mark” in italic, an icon made of six W's and a little postage stamp with the community's name.

My part

I drew this logo myself, from the first page of sketches to the final files. With the team at Zipcode Creative, I also wrote the creative direction and built the visual identity and the 19-page brand guide. I selected the brand photography, too.

“Effortlessly cool, playfully confident, approachable and friendly, charming.”

“Nothing too feminine, frilly, romanticized”On style

The concepts

Three concepts, one choice

I presented three concepts, each with its own logo. The client chose Concept 1, Leaving a Mark, which plays on the name itself.

  1. Concept 1The chosen one Leaving a Mark Identity and signature, playing on the name.
  2. Concept 2 Arch and Belonging Inspired by the building: the arch as a symbol of welcome.
  3. Concept 3 Effortlessly Cool Loud, fun and chunky, but still charming.

The logo

A name that leaves a mark

I filled pages with ways to write Watermark, from stamps to monograms, before landing on a lowercase wordmark with “mark” in italic and an icon made of six W's. Four rounds, one week in February.

The final brand

Warm colors and wavy lines

Six colors, from deep Oxide to bright Saffron. Wavy lines, cross-hatching and a little postage stamp with the community's name carry the rest.
