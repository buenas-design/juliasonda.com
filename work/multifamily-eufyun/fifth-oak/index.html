Fifth Oak

  • Creative direction

  • Brand photography

The project

Fifth Oak is a new rental community in northeast Raleigh, North Carolina: 392 homes on 50 acres, with apartments, townhomes and cottages in one planned neighborhood.

The challenge

The same brand had to welcome someone renting a first apartment and a family moving into a townhome. It had to feel modern and warm at once, close to the trails and close to the city. And the client set one rule for the icon: no literal tree.

The idea

So we built a tree out of triangles. The shapes come together as an oak and break apart into patterns for every layout, the colors follow the different woods the client hoped for, and a five-leaf branch carries the number in the name.

My part

With the team at Zipcode Creative, I wrote the creative direction, directed the logo and built the visual identity around the triangles, all the way to the 29-page brand guide. I selected the brand photography, too.

“We are a fit for customers who are looking for a rental lifestyle BUT that rental is also set within a planned community.”

What they asked for

“No ‘literal’ tree icon included”On the icon

“Generally more modern feel mixed with organic materials… Warm and inviting urban lodge”On the feel

“Intersection of local outdoor recreation AND close proximity to the action of city life”On the place

“We were also interested in possibly including an interpretation of ‘different colors of wood’…”On color

“You can walk your dog within the community for 30 minutes without leaving the property”On the land

The concepts

Three concepts, one choice

I presented three concepts, each with its own logo and layouts. The client chose Concept 3, Triangle.

  1. Concept 1 Geometric A hexagon system inspired by beehives.
  2. Concept 2 Nature The growth rings of an oak, a place you grow into.
  3. Concept 3The chosen one Triangle A tree built from triangles, four outlined and one solid.

The chosen one · Concept 3

Triangle

“Deconstructed trees built from triangle shapes, reinforcing the geometric foundation.”

Harmonic · Natural · Balanced · Warm

The logo

Five leaves on one branch

I wrote the direction for the logo: a modern tree, with the number five tucked into its branches or leaves. The five-leaf branch first showed up in the hexagon concept, and it was the one that stayed next to the triangles.

The final brand

The colors of wood

Six colors, from deep green to rust and cream, close to the different colors of wood the client hoped for. The triangles that build the tree also break apart into patterns for every layout.
