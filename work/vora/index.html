Vora

Lifestyle · Packaging · Branding · Print & Collateral

Vora makes aromatic candles by hand, with 100% vegetable wax, vegan and made in small batches. The brand needed to feel as calm and natural as the candles themselves.

I created the identity and the candle labels, one for each scent, from Camomila to Amora, and carried it into the stationery, stickers, tote and digital pieces.
