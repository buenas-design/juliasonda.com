Tell Me What You Need

Branding & Visual Identity

Branding is my favorite kind of work, and it's a big part of what I do.

I work on visual identities for businesses in all kinds of industries. Sometimes an agency comes to me with a creative direction already in place. Other times, I'm involved from the first concepts.

I can develop the visual identity and prepare the guidelines and files your team needs to present and deliver the brand.

Brand concepts · Logos · Visual identities · Brand guidelines · Brand applications

Creative Direction for Brands

Sometimes a brand needs someone to set the direction before anything gets designed.

I can take care of that part: define the look and feel, put together the visual references, and make sure the logo, the social media, the packaging and everything else feel like they belong to the same brand.

It works for new brands and for brands that already exist but feel a bit scattered.

Graphic & Marketing Design

A lot of the agencies I work with need help beyond branding, too.

I design presentations, social media graphics, brochures, flyers, packaging, and other marketing materials. I also work with existing brand guidelines, so there's no need to reinvent anything when the direction is already established.

I also do website design and motion, like animated logos, project videos and reels, following the same brand guidelines as the rest of the work.

Social media · Presentations · Digital assets · Print · Packaging · Marketing collateral · Website design · Motion

Ongoing Design Support

I work with agencies that send me projects regularly, and I really enjoy that kind of relationship. After a while, I get to know the team, the kinds of clients they work with, and what they expect from me. We don't have to explain everything again with each new project.

If design work keeps landing on your team every month, a monthly design retainer is usually the easiest way to work together. You book the hours you need, send me tasks as they come in, and get one invoice at the end of the month. The more hours you book, the lower my hourly rate.

I also work by the hour or per project, so there's no need to commit to something ongoing before we've worked together.

Monthly retainers · Hourly design support · Project-based design · White-label design for agencies
