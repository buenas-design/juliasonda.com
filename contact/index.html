FAQ

  1. 01

    Do you work completely white-label?

    Yes. I can work directly with your agency without being involved in client communication. I also won't share your projects publicly without permission.

  2. 02

    Can I hire you for a single project?

    Of course! A lot of agency partnerships start with one project. We can talk about ongoing work later if it makes sense.

  3. 03

    Can you follow our creative direction?

    Yes. I'm used to working with creative directors and existing brand guidelines. I can also develop the visual direction when that's part of the project.

  4. 04

    How do you charge?

    I offer fixed project fees and hourly pricing, depending on the work. Send me the details and I'll prepare an estimate.

  5. 05

    Can we work together if we're in different countries?

    Absolutely. I'm based in Spain and I've worked with clients in 9 countries, most of them in the US and Canada.
